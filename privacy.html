Privacy & cookies

How we use cookies on this site, what we collect, and how to turn it off.

Last updated August 3, 2026

The short version

We use cookies to understand how people find our events and to show our ads to people who have already visited this site. We do not sell your personal information. We never put your email or phone number into a URL, and ticket payments happen on Posh, not here, so this site never sees your card details.

Cookies we set

CookiePurposeLasts
dfl_vidA random ID so we can tell a returning visitor from a new one. It is not linked to your name.2 years
dfl_firstRemembers how you first found us (the ad, link, or search that brought you) so we know which promotion is working.2 years
_fbp, _fbcSet by the Meta (Facebook and Instagram) advertising pixel. Lets us show our event ads to people who have visited this site, and measure whether those ads led to ticket sales.Up to 90 days

Our page-view analytics run on a self-hosted Umami instance. It sets no cookies and does not follow you across other websites. Each page view sends the page address, the referring page, your screen size, and your browser language, and Umami turns those plus your IP address into a daily rotating anonymous ID that cannot be traced back to you or linked across days.

What we collect when you join The List

We receive your email address, your phone number if you choose to add one, and which event you were looking at, plus the cookie values above so we know which promotion brought you. We use it to tell you about upcoming events. Reply STOP to any text to opt out.

What we collect when you request a gear rental

The rental form asks for your name, email, and phone number, all of them required, along with your event date, the equipment you want, whether you need pickup or delivery, and whether you are taking the damage waiver or providing your own certificate of insurance. It also records that you ticked the box accepting responsibility for the equipment while it is in your care. That acknowledgment is kept as part of the rental record.

We use your rental contact details to answer that rental request and to arrange the rental. We do not add them to our event mailing or texting list. The cookie values above are attached so we can see which promotions bring in rental enquiries.

How to turn tracking off

This site honors the Global Privacy Control signal and Do Not Track. If your browser sends either one, the Meta advertising pixel is never loaded, no advertising cookies are set, and our analytics stop recording your visits. The only cookie still used is dfl_vid, which just tells a returning visitor from a new one and is not used for advertising. You can enable Global Privacy Control in browsers such as Firefox, Brave, and DuckDuckGo, or through a privacy extension.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time, and you can control how Meta uses your activity in your Facebook or Instagram ad preferences. Blocking cookies will not stop you from browsing the site or buying tickets.

Who else receives data

Meta Platforms receives the advertising pixel events described above. Posh processes ticket sales on its own checkout and under its own privacy policy. Our form submissions are stored on our own infrastructure. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Your choices

If you are a California resident you may ask us not to share your information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If you are in the UK or EU you may ask for a copy of your data or ask us to delete it. Email us and we will handle it.
